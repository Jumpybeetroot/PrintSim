## VzBot 330 AWD Full Build: Gantry-on-Top-Frame Topology & Bolted Bed Spacers (2026-11-26)

Ground truth: official VzBoT3D `Vz-330_v1.2` full-printer STEP (Z-up, `-Y` front), BOM, `Firmware/330AWD-printer.cfg`, and the cited community Klipper `[input_shaper]` results in `docs/community_touchstones.md`.

- **Gantry plane on the top ring (`frame.gantry_on_top_frame`)**: VzBot bolts the MGN12H Y rails to the *inner* faces of the top-ring 2020 Y extrusions (CAD rail `X = ±245..±237`, `Z = 39..51`, top-ring centerline `Z = 45`). The default fixed-gantry topology always places a second 2020 ring at least `gantry_top_clearance_mm ≥ 25 mm` below the top ring with short upright stubs, a phantom double ring that over-stiffens top-frame bending/torsion. With the flag, `z_gantry = h`, the `col_*_top` stubs and the duplicate `top_left*/top_right*/top_rear` members are removed, and each side becomes one continuous `y_rail_{s}_*` chain `g_f → t_rod_f → (t_left_mid) → y_rail_car → t_rod_r → g_r` (rail composite applied to every segment). Coincident `t_*`/`g_*` corners are joined by 6-DOF penalty ties $K = \mathrm{diag}(10^{10}, 10^{10}, 10^{10}\,\mathrm{N/m}, 10^{9}, 10^{9}, 10^{9}\,\mathrm{N{\cdot}m/rad})$, the same order as the `rigid` corner-joint reference, so panels, braces and modal frame metrics that address `t_*` keep working. Removing the double ring strictly lowers frame extrusion mass (`tests/test_vzbot_330.py`).
- **Per-side panel sheets (`frame.side_panel_material`)**: left/right MITC4 bays take their own membrane stiffness $E t$, ACM sandwich terms, clamp efficiency and areal mass; the rear/top keep `panel_material`. VzBot: 3 mm 5052 rear + 3 mm polycarbonate sides. Softer side sheets can only lower the Y-racking shaper frequency (monotonic test).
- **Bolted silicone spacers (`model_overrides.bed_mount`, VzBot preset)**: each 16 mm 70A spacer is clamped by an M3×50 countersunk screw (plate → spacer → 2020 bed frame → knob), a preloaded joint where the screw carries external load in parallel with the elastomer. Axial: $k_b = EA_s/L = 200\,\mathrm{GPa}\cdot 4.5\,\mathrm{mm^2}/50\,\mathrm{mm} \approx 18\,\mathrm{kN/mm}$, capped by knob/frame flex to 2,000 N/mm. Lateral: M3 minor-diameter shank bending over the 16 mm spacer, $3EI/L^3 \ldots 12EI/L^3 = 235 \ldots 940$ N/mm (400 N/mm used). XY shaper results are insensitive across that range (X 105.8–105.9 Hz, Y 65.0–65.5 Hz), whereas the free-elastomer catalog values (48 / 16 N/mm) let bed-sway modes contaminate the bed-relative carriage receptance (Y 48.8 Hz). The generic `silicone_4pt` catalog entry is unchanged.
- **Z-stack clearance for gantry-on-top-frame machines (`kinematics/collision.py`)**: parts above the X beam live in the hinged top hat, so the generic `85 mm + ½·envelope_z` heuristic is replaced by the explicit stack bottom ring + bed carriage (PEI 1 + plate + standoff + sub-frame profile + 35 mm bearing-mount drop) + build Z + nozzle drop (+6 mm only for front-datum toolheads on a top rail) + ½ top-ring height. VzBot 330: $20 + 80 + 400 + 54.2 + 10 = 564.2 \le 570$ mm (7.8 mm spare, matching the STEP's 7.8 mm bed-carriage-to-bottom-ring gap at Z = 400).
- **CAD-measured toolhead datums (`vz_printhead_cnc`)**: printhead CoMs from the STEP re-weighted with real part masses (27 g CNC plates, 47 g HextrudORT Low, 85 g Moons NEMA14, 26 g MGN9H, printed CPAP duct/mount volumes, 2510 fan, Beacon) give 247 g on-rail + 96 g Goliath Air = 343 g, assembled CoG `[+1.9, −10.3, +13.4]` mm and Goliath tip `[−0.1, −22.4, −54.2]` mm about the CF tube axis. The preset stores these CAD-true values with the new `toolhead.offset_datum_rail_mount = "top"`: the builder's legacy top/bottom-mount datum shift (−6 mm |Y|, −6 mm Z, meant for front-carriage presets moved onto a top rail) is applied only when `gantry.x_rail_mount` differs from the datum (moving the Vz-Printhead to a front rail first undoes the top datum), so the realized `toolhead_cog` / `nozzle_tip` nodes land exactly on the CAD values (`toolhead_chassis` T-branch, since the CoG is above and the nozzle below the rail).
- **Inner-face Y-rail attachment (raceway pivot)**: on `y_rail_mount: inner` the moving carriage node `y_car_*` now sits on the ball-raceway line, $r = \tfrac{1}{2}b_{\text{extrusion}} + h_{\text{raceway}}$ inboard of the Y-extrusion axis (`y_carriage_raceway_offset_m`; MGN12 on 2020: $10 + 4.8 = 14.8$ mm, VzBot CAD ≈ 15 mm). The Hertzian `y_car_bearing_*` acts at the raceway with a rigid arm `y_rail_car − y_car` back to the extrusion axis (replacing the lumped 7.5 mm raceway-to-bolt-face lever on inner rails), so carriage roll pivots about the real contact line and the XY-joint superelement spans raceway → CF-tube clamp (28.7 mm on VzBot) instead of axis → clamp (43.5 mm). Top/bottom mounts keep the lumped lever. Effect: VzBot Y 65.1 → 64.9 Hz; Monolith AWD stays at 154.8 / 99.2 Hz.
- **Printed vs CNC Vz-Printhead superelements**: both heads are Tet4 Craig–Bampton superelements on the same official cage datums (Port 0 = MGN9H block top face, 20 mm above the tube axis; Port 1 = Goliath top face at $(-0.06, -22.36, -5.0)$ mm). `vz_printhead_printed` is the exact printed v24 cage from the STEP (top 10.26 + back 8.69 + bottom 8.22 + front 3.25 + adapter 2.23 cm³, 4× M3 standoffs) in ABS with the 0.32× printed multi-piece knockdown. `vz_printhead_cnc` uses the same layout with 1.5 mm top/bottom and 2.5 mm side 6061 plates whose windows are fitted so the set reproduces **both** of Mellow's published masses: $\rho_{Al}(V_{tb} + V_s) = 27$ g and $\rho_{CF} V_{tb} + \rho_{Al} V_s = 22$ g (CF top/bottom hybrid), giving 27.4 g / 22.1 g. It is meshed conformally with Gmsh and gets the 0.58× bolted-seam knockdown via the new `multi_piece_bolted` manifest flag. The Goliath stays a visual-only hotend GLB; with its 69.24 mm length the drawn nozzle lands at $20 - 5 - 69.24 = -54.24$ mm, matching the FEA nozzle node.
- **Populated rail-fastener pitch (`gantry.x_rail_bolt_pitch_mm`, `y_rail_bolt_pitch_mm`)**: the Newmark partial-composite slip stiffness of a bolted rail uses the spacing of the screws actually installed, not the rail's hole pitch. VzBot: 6× M3 into in-tube nuts on the MGN9 (every 4th 20 mm hole, CAD x = ±30/110/190 → 80 mm) and every other 25 mm hole on the MGN12 Y rails (manual → 50 mm). The X-rail composite factor drops from $\gamma = 0.54$ (25 mm) to $0.27$; shapers move X 106.0 → 105.9, Y 64.9 → 64.5 Hz; the BOM's rail screws fall from 51 to 23 (official BOM: "M3 8mm ×20 or more"). Unset fields fall back to the shared `rail_bolt_pitch_mm`, so every other preset is unchanged.
- **Belt tension**: VzBot tensions 6 mm GT2 to 150–189 Hz over a 150 mm span; $T = 4L^2 f^2 \mu$ with Gates 6 mm $\mu = 8.3$ g/m gives 16.8–26.7 N (22 N ≈ 171 Hz used).
