# PrintSim Model Confidence, Calibration Ledger & Engineering References

> **Purpose (Living Backend Document for AI Agents & Engineers):**
> This document is the single backend ledger tracking **every modeled and lumped physical parameter in PrintSim**, its **0–100% engineering confidence score**, its **exact source/datasheet/paper references**, the **code symbol where it lives**, and the **specific bench test, ADXL345 log, or 3D CAD FEA study needed to raise its trust score over time**.
>
> It stays **strictly synchronized** with the live Studio **WIP · Model Trust?** modal powered by `ITEM_CONFIDENCE_REGISTRY` in [`src/printsim/schema/confidence.py`](../src/printsim/schema/confidence.py) and `build_model_confidence_audit()` in [`src/printsim/schema/overrides.py`](../src/printsim/schema/overrides.py).

---

## 1. Confidence Tier Scale (0–100%)

| Tier | Range | Meaning | How to Use Results |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`exact`** (Datasheet / Exact) | **90–100%** | Closed-form textbook mechanics, verified FEM patch tests, or manufacturer CAD/alloy datasheets with <3% variance. | Safe to trust quantitatively (given accurate upstream inputs). |
| **`calibrated`** (Empirical / Calibrated) | **70–89%** | First-principles physics paired with manufacturer specs and calibrated against real-world ADXL345 logs or bench data. | Accurate within ~10–15% across comparable builds; verify edge cases. |
| **`estimate`** (Lumped Estimate) | **50–69%** | Reduced-order / lumped spring-damper representation of unmodeled hardware (bolted joints, printed brackets, Tap rails, feet). | Reliable for directional A/B trends; override in Advanced Mode if measured. |
| **`heuristic`** (Rough Heuristic / Theoretical) | **< 50%** | Uncalibrated multi-part assembly heuristic (e.g., toolhead body construction) or theoretical industrial datasheet extrapolation unproven on 3D printers (e.g., AT3/AT5 belts). | Treat as rough order-of-magnitude only; top priority for future bench testing or CAD FEA. |

## 4. Priority Calibration Queue (Biggest Accuracy Gains First)

When an AI agent or human engineer has time to improve PrintSim's fidelity, work from the top of this list downward:

1. **Toolhead Body Construction (`45%` → target `75%+`)**: Run `tet_fem.py` on official STEP CAD of StealthBurner, XOL, A4T, Dragon Burner, and Archetype (plus 10 N static tip-deflection bench measurements) to replace heuristic `(body_stiffness_base, torsion_mult)`.
2. **Theoretical Industrial Belts (`40%` → target `75%+`) & Proven 2GT Dynamic Factor (`80%` → `90%`)**: Collect dynamic small-amplitude $EA(f, T_0)$ bench curves for Gates 2GT 6/9/12 mm and real CoreXY ADXL data if/when 3GT or AT3 builds exist.
3. **Z-Probe Tap Compliance (`52%` → target `80%+`)**: Archive controlled same-machine ADXL345 CSV pairs (Printed Tap vs CNC Tap vs Static Eddy/Beacon) and replace the scalar `compliance_factor` knockdown with an explicit sliding-rail pitch/translation spring element.
4. **Lumped Gantry Brackets (`54%` → target `80%+`)**: Pre-condense Craig–Bampton CMS superelements (`superelement.py`) for stock Voron 2.4/Trident printed XY-joints & LDO Monolith CNC XY-joints across ABS, PPA-CF, and 6061-T6.
5. **Benchtop Feet (`54%` → target `75%+`) & Frame Corner Joints (`60%` → target `80%+`)**: Measure static force-deflection on common feet (rubber, HULA, paver) and 2020 corner joints (blind joint, L-bracket, gusset plate, corner cube).
6. **Hotend Clamp & Soft Bed Mounts (`50–55%` → target `75%+`)**: Static nozzle-tip load tests across clamp styles and hot `BED_MESH_OUTPUT` thermal-taco sweeps across bed mount types.
